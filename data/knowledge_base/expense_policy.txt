EXPENSE REIMBURSEMENT POLICY — ACME CORP (SAMPLE / FICTIONAL)
Policy Reference: FIN-003 | Version 1.8 | Last Updated: February 2025
This is a fictional sample document for demonstration purposes only.

=============================================================
SECTION 1: OVERVIEW
=============================================================

This policy governs the reimbursement of business-related expenses incurred by
Acme Corp employees. All expenses must be legitimate, reasonable, and directly
related to company business.

=============================================================
SECTION 2: ELIGIBLE EXPENSES
=============================================================

2.1 Business Travel
- Airfare: Economy class for domestic flights under 6 hours; business class permitted for international
- Ground transportation: Taxis, rideshare, rental cars (compact class)
- Accommodation: Up to $200 USD per night for domestic; up to $300 per night for international
- Meals during travel: Up to $50 USD per day domestic; up to $75 per day international

2.2 Client Entertainment
- Meals with clients: Up to $100 per person with prior manager approval
- Client events, tickets, or activities: Require VP-level pre-approval
- Alcohol: Permitted only as part of client entertainment; not reimbursed for team meals

2.3 Professional Development
- Conference registration fees: Up to $2,000 per conference with manager approval
- Training courses or certifications: Up to $1,500 per year without additional approval
- Books and subscriptions directly related to role: Up to $200 per year

2.4 Office Supplies (Remote Workers)
- Home office equipment under $100: Reimbursed without prior approval
- Home office equipment $100-$500: Requires manager approval
- Home office annual stipend: $500 per year (see Employee Handbook Section 4.5)

=============================================================
SECTION 3: EXPENSE SUBMISSION PROCESS
=============================================================

3.1 Submission Portal
All expenses must be submitted through the Expense Portal at expenses.acmecorp.example.com

3.2 Submission Deadline
Expenses must be submitted within 30 calendar days of the expense date.
Late submissions may be rejected at manager discretion.

3.3 Required Documentation
- Itemized receipt required for all expenses over $25
- Receipts must show: vendor name, date, amount, and items purchased
- Credit card statements alone are not acceptable substitutes for itemized receipts

3.4 Approval Workflow
1. Employee submits expense with receipt and business justification
2. Direct manager reviews and approves (or rejects with reason)
3. Finance team processes approved expenses
4. Reimbursement issued within 14 business days of final approval

=============================================================
SECTION 4: NON-REIMBURSABLE EXPENSES
=============================================================

The following expenses will NOT be reimbursed:
- Personal travel or entertainment
- Traffic fines, parking tickets, or penalties
- Lost or stolen personal items
- Expenses for spouses, partners, or family members (unless pre-approved)
- Upgrades beyond policy limits (e.g., first class when business class is the maximum)
- Expenses incurred after employee resignation or termination
- Expenses without valid receipts (for amounts over $25)

=============================================================
SECTION 5: CORPORATE CREDIT CARD
=============================================================

5.1 Eligibility
Corporate credit cards are issued to employees who travel more than 4 times per year.
Applications must be approved by Finance and HR.

5.2 Usage Rules
- Corporate cards must only be used for approved business expenses
- Personal charges on corporate cards require immediate reimbursement
- Cards must be reported lost or stolen within 24 hours

=============================================================
END OF DOCUMENT
